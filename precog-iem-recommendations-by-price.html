Precog’s IEM Recommendations by Price

My personal picks from sub-$50 to unlimited-budget flagships. These are recommendations based on listening experience, not simply the highest-scoring entries on the ranking list.

Under $50

Don’t delude yourself into thinking you’re getting good sound. But we’re also past the dark ages of 5–6 years ago when manufacturers were shooting in the dark. Most modern sub-$50 IEMs sound decent, albeit pretty similar, and there’s not much point in overthinking your purchase at this price.

Good if you want bass. The upper-midrange and treble can be a bit grainy sounding and it comes accompanied by a pretty cheap build, though.

Under $200

Okay, here’s where things actually start getting good if you pick the right IEMs.

Juzear Light Shuttle

My pick for the best overall package under $200. Think of this as a warmer, significantly cheaper Softears RSV, the IEM that predicted the current JM-1 tuning trend. In my opinion, the Light Shuttle’s the best JM-1 IEM on the market and largely clears alternatives like the CrinEar Daybreak, NICEHCK Rockies, Mega5EST, and KiwiEars KE4, although you might prefer some of these other options if you want less bass. Either way, I would buy this IEM with my own money.

Under $500

$500’s the new $1000. I wouldn’t have believed it just a year ago, but some of these new IEMs come scarily close to the level of sound quality that you’d previously only get from flagship IEMs, and I wouldn’t mind daily-driving some of them. If you’re serious about portable audio and can stretch your budget a little, then I think this price category is the current sweet spot for price to performance.

YU9 Audio Que

My best overall pick in this bracket, especially if you want flagship-style tuning without the flagship price tag. This IEM’s tuning is inspired by heavyweights like the 64 Audio U12t, qdc Anole VX, and Elysian Annihilator. It doesn’t quite measure up for raw technicalities compared to some of these aforementioned IEMs but it’s not far off.

A lot of these IEMs are older and their place in the hierarchy hasn’t changed dramatically to my ears even as their cheaper brethren have made great strides. However, because of that, I feel like the IEMs at this price point are more difficult to recommend as the performance gains become increasingly marginal.

Unlimited Budget

Okay, so you’re a big baller. These are the IEMs that I think are at the forefront of the IEM world. Be aware that at this price point, adherence to tuning convention begins diverging even more and what you’re mostly paying for are unique flavors of sound and subjective technical qualities.

Subtonic Storm

The king of colored clarity. Still the best implementation of this presentation I’ve heard, the Storm is exceptionally micro-detailed with a presentation that is highly sensitive to source pairing.
