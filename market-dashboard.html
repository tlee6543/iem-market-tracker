IEM Secondary Market at a Glance

This page uses the full tracker dataset to show market-wide pricing, activity, and resale trends. The index is designed to answer a different question from an individual IEM page: is the broader used-IEM market moving up or down?

The market index is anchored to 100 in January 2024. It is equal-weighted across IEMs and uses same-IEM month-to-month price changes, rather than simply averaging all sale prices, so a changing mix of cheap and expensive products has less influence on the trend.

How the Market Index Works

1. One price per IEM per month

Clean closed sales are collapsed to a monthly median for each IEM. A frequently traded model therefore does not automatically dominate the market.

2. Compare the same IEM over time

The index looks at price changes for an IEM between observations up to six months apart, monthlyizes that change, and rejects extreme 4× / 0.25× jumps as likely bad data.

3. Median market move

Each month's move is the median same-IEM change across at least 5 contributors. The moves are compounded from a January 2024 value of 100.

This is an analytical index, not an investable price index. Condition, accessories, regional differences, sample depth, and the exact IEMs trading in a month can still affect the result.
