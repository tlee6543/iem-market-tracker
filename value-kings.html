Value Kings

Price efficiency, adjusted for cheaper alternatives

This page estimates the price normally associated with each Final Score in Precogvision's formal ranking list. Unlike the previous model, it predicts log10(Price) from Final Score, so an IEM is rewarded for costing less than its score would normally imply rather than merely outperforming other products in an expensive price bracket.

Value Multiple = Expected Price ÷ Actual Price. A result of 2.00× means the IEM costs about half of the model-implied price for its score. That efficiency is standardized, then reduced when a substantially cheaper IEM scores nearly as well.

Adjusted Value Score = Price-Efficiency σ − 0.75 × log10(Comparable Premium). The comparable is the cheapest lower-priced IEM scoring no more than 0.25 points below the current IEM. Higher-scoring cheaper IEMs also qualify. Only products priced below their model expectation and finishing with a positive adjusted score are shown.

Formal rankings with a valid Final Score and ranking-sheet Price are included. Demo-only entries, resale retention, fit, accessories, availability, and listener preference are not part of this model. See Resale Leaders for secondary-market retention.
